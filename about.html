About Our School

Benchmark International School (BIS) is a modern educational institution located in Tongi, Gazipur, Bangladesh. Established in 2022, the school was founded with the goal of providing quality education and creating a learning environment where students can grow academically, socially, and morally. The institution offers education from Playgroup to Secondary School Certificate (SSC) level. The school follows the National Curriculum of Bangladesh and provides instruction in both Bangla and English mediums. Benchmark International School believes that education should not only focus on academic excellence but also on character building, creativity, leadership, and confidence. The school encourages students to participate in various extracurricular activities including cultural programs, sports, debates, and creative competitions.
